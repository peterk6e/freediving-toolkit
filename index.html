⚠️ Safety Guidelines

Essential safety practices for freediving and apnea training.

🚨 CRITICAL SAFETY REMINDERS
  • Never freedive alone - Always have a trained buddy present
  • Train with certified instructors - Professional guidance is essential
  • Monitor for shallow water blackout - Loss of consciousness can be fatal
  • Avoid hyperventilation - It reduces CO₂ signals and increases blackout risk
  • Respect your limits - Progressive training prevents injuries
  • Know mammalian dive reflex - Understand your body's responses at depth
  • Maintain fitness - Good cardiovascular health is crucial
  • Check equipment regularly - Proper gear prevents accidents

Pre-Dive Checklist

Complete before every session.

Emergency Procedures

Know what to do in emergencies.

If buddy loses consciousness:
1. Bring them to surface immediately
2. Clear airway
3. Begin rescue breathing if trained
4. Call emergency services

  • Static Apnea (STA) - Breath holding on the surface

  • Constant Weight (CWT) - Weighted dive to depth
