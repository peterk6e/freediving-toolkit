Safety Notes

⚠ Freediving involves serious risks including blackout, loss of motor control, and drowning.

  • Educational tool only. This toolkit is not a substitute for certified training and supervision.
  • Never train alone. Always dive with a trained partner and follow established safety protocols.
  • No medical device. This tool does not predict or prevent blackout or any medical emergency.
  • Hyperventilation risk. Never hyperventilate before breath-holds.
  • Follow your coach. Your instructor's guidance always supersedes any calculator or table.
