⚠️ Safety Guidelines

Essential safety practices and protocols every freediver must know.

⚠️ Shallow Water Blackout (SWB)

Never hyperventilate before breath holding. Keep training in controlled environments with a partner. Sudden loss of consciousness can happen without warning.

The Buddy System

Always dive with a trained partner who can respond in emergencies.

  • Maintain eye contact underwater
  • Use agreed-upon hand signals
  • Establish safety limits beforehand
  • Never exceed your training level

Emergency Response

Know CPR and recovery procedures.

  • Bring unconscious diver to surface
  • Place on back and tilt head back
  • Begin CPR if not breathing
  • Call emergency services immediately

Pre-Dive Checklist

Always perform before entering the water.

  • Equipment inspection
  • Buddy briefing
  • Surface interval time
  • No hyperventilation
  • Calm mental state

📚 About This Toolkit

The Freediving Training Toolkit is an educational resource designed to support freediving training, education, and performance improvement. It provides physics-based calculators, training table generators, and tracking tools for both recreational and competitive freedivers.

Key Features:

  • Physics calculators (pressure, lung compression, gas laws)
  • CO₂ and O₂ table generators for structured breathing training
  • Static apnea timer for breath-hold practice
  • Performance tracking and dive logging
  • Training schedule planner
  • Safety guidelines and emergency protocols
  • Educational resources and certifications

Who Is This For?

  • Recreational freediving enthusiasts learning proper techniques
  • Competitive apnea athletes preparing for competitions
  • Freediving instructors teaching new students
  • Anyone interested in apnea physiology and breath-hold training

Safety Reminder:

Always train with a certified instructor and a trained buddy. Never freedive alone. Shallow water blackout can occur suddenly and without warning. This toolkit is for educational purposes and training support only—not a substitute for professional instruction or medical advice.

Topics Covered:

  • Freediving basics and techniques
  • Apnea training methods
  • Physics and pressure calculations
  • Breathing tables (CO₂ and O₂ training)
  • Safety protocols and emergency response
  • Equipment selection and maintenance
  • Dive planning and depth training
  • Training periodization and progression
  • Mental preparation and relaxation
  • Competition preparation
