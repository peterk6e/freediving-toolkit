About this project

Freediving Training Toolkit is a lightweight educational web application designed to support freedivers in training structure, basic physics understanding, and simple performance tracking.

Purpose

This tool is built to help with:
  • Structuring freediving training sessions
  • Understanding basic pressure and volume physics
  • Planning CO₂ and O₂ tables
  • Tracking personal dives locally
It is not a coaching system and does not replace certified training.

What this is NOT

  • Not a medical tool
  • Not a safety system
  • Not a physiological risk model
  • Not a replacement for freediving instructors

Core principles

Simplicity over complexity. Clarity over simulation. Structure over prediction.

Safety reminder

Freediving carries real risk including blackout and drowning. Always train with proper supervision and certification.
